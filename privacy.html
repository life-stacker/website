Who we are

Life Stacker is run by Life Stacker Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17543864). We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. You can contact us about your details at hello@life-stacker.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • If you choose to tell us: how many children you have, your town or area, and whether you'd like to be considered as a test family.
  • The date you joined.

We don't ask for your children's names or ages here.

Why we use it

To email you about Life Stacker testing and launch, to invite you to apply to be a test family, and to invite you to become a founding family. The optional answers help us choose a good mix of test families and decide where to launch first.

We use your details because you've agreed to it (consent). You can withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing hello@life-stacker.com.

Who can see it

Only Life Stacker Ltd. We never sell your details or share them with advertisers. We use these companies to run the website, and they only handle your details on our instructions:

  • Supabase stores the waiting list on servers in London.
  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any website host, it briefly handles your device's internet address when you visit.
  • When we send emails to the list we'll use an email service and name it here first.

Where a company handles details outside the UK, it does so under the safeguards UK law requires.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove you, or until 12 months after Life Stacker launches if you haven't become a customer, whichever is sooner. If you ask us to remove you, we'll do it within 30 days.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete them, or withdraw your consent, by emailing hello@life-stacker.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
