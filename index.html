There's always one person in a family with forty tabs open in their head.

Who's doing pickup on Tuesday when you're stuck at work, which child has swimming and which has drama, whose PE kit needs washing, and who on earth has the kids at half term.

Good at it or not, it falls to them. And it never switches off. It's there at eleven at night, again at six in the morning, and halfway through your kid's bedtime story.

Life Stacker takes it out of your head and into one shared plan. Everyone knows where they need to be, and nothing gets left behind.

Know who's got the kids

Every grown up, child and carer on one plan. No more "what's happening tomorrow?" Everyone can see it for themselves.

Stay on top of every run and holiday

Every drop off and pickup has a name next to it, and whoever's going gets reminded what to bring. School holidays stop being a puzzle you solve in a panic.

Spot clashes before they happen

Both of you needing the car, a pickup in the middle of a meeting, a holiday day with nobody covering. You see it days ahead, not at 3pm.

And the load is finally shared.

Your partner

Sees what's coming and can pick things up, so it's not all down to one of you.

The kids

Know their own day, what to pack and what to tick off, so you're not the one chasing.

School emails

Shrink to the one line that matters most to your child. No more wading through class WhatsApps.

Picture a normal Tuesday. The kids know who's picking them up. The swimming bag is by the door because someone was reminded last night. Thursday's clash was sorted days ago. And when your phone buzzes at bedtime, it isn't another question you have to answer.

Be one of our founding families

Life Stacker is being built by a mum of two who was that person: drowning in work and logistics, with everyone asking her what was happening tomorrow. A small group of families will test it first and help decide what comes next.
