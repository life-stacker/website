There's always one person in a family with forty tabs open in their head.

Who's doing pickup on Tuesday when you're stuck at work, which child has swimming and which has drama, whose PE kit needs washing, and who on earth has the kids at half term.

Good at it or not, it falls to them. And it never switches off. It's there at eleven at night, again at six in the morning, and halfway through your kid's bedtime story.

Life Stacker takes it out of your head and into one shared plan. Everyone knows where they need to be, and nothing gets left behind.

Built by that person, for that person

Somewhere between work and the school run, I became the person everyone asked, "What's happening tomorrow?" I wanted a way for the whole family to know, not just me. So I'm building Life Stacker, for my family and for yours.

A small group of families will test it first and help decide what comes next.

Everyone on the waiting list will be invited to become one of our founding families, keeping a lower price for as long as they stay.
